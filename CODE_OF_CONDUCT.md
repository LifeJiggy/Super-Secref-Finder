# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Super Secref Finder to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Redact real credentials and personal data from issues, logs, and pull requests.
- Respect responsible disclosure and do not publish another person's secrets.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and move discussion to a new issue when
  a topic is out of scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing live credentials, customer data, or scan results from systems you do
  not own.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who
violate this code. Report concerns privately to <Bloomtonjovish@gmail.com>.
Reports are handled confidentially.
